## 🔬 Current Research Direction

### Human–AI Coevolution in Digital Communities

My central research question is:

> **How do AI-powered recommender systems and human behavior coevolve to reshape information diffusion, trust formation, and participation in digital communities?**

I am especially interested in studying the feedback relationship between people and intelligent systems:

**Human behavior → AI learning → Algorithmic recommendations → Human behavior**

Users shape AI systems through clicks, searches, comments, shares, preferences, and attention. Those systems then influence what users see, whom they interact with, and which information becomes visible.

I want to use **Network Science** to better understand these interactions and contribute to more transparent, trustworthy, and human-centered AI systems.
